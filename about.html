About Tachbonest Classic Ventures

A registered Lagos business that supplies and installs window blinds, and buys food items for customers from their list.

What we do

We supply vertical and Venetian blinds for homes, offices, shops and churches. You give us the window sizes and the colours you like, and we send a written quotation. Installation can be added to the job.

We also run a food buying service. You send the list of items and quantities you need, we confirm the current prices with you, and we buy the items for you.

How we work

  • Every blind job starts with a quotation that lists each window, its width and drop, the colour, and the price.
  • Food prices move often, so we confirm prices with you before we buy.
  • You can reach us by phone, WhatsApp, or through the order pages on this website.

Conditions of service

These are printed on every quotation.

  • Prices are subject to change without notice.
  • Payment is made before service.
  • The time to complete the job (in days or weeks) is written on the quotation.
  • The client agrees with all the particulars on the quotation by signing it.
